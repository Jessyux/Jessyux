200–1,000 m Mesopelagic · Twilight How I work

I am the person who maps what is under the interface.

I am Jessica Chappell, a product designer and product manager at Montu, working on uMeds ordering and fulfilment. I pull from journey discovery, voice of customer and data before I make a call, and I think in systems and dependencies.

01

Evidence before opinion

Two datasets before a direction. Research discovery, VOC and analytics decide the brief, not the loudest person in the room.

02

Design the chain, not the screen

A tap in checkout is a regulated operational sequence underneath. I map it with the teams who own each link, before the design is final.

03

Stay for the measurement

Adoption, change management and post-implementation tracking are part of the design. I report the misses as clearly as the wins.

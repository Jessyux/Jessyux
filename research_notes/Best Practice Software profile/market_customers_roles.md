# Best Practice Software: market, competitors, customers, regulation and product roles (as of 29 Oct 2026)

Note on sourcing: ~22 searches/fetches. Several Employment Hero job ads returned 404 (closed). No Reddit, Capterra or ProductReview user reviews of Bp Premier surfaced. Market share figures are estimates only; no audited figure exists in what I found.

## Market structure and market share

### Takeaway
Best Practice (Bp Premier) is the clear leader in Australian GP clinical/practice management software, with published estimates from ~60% to ~80% of GP practices, and MedicalDirector (Telstra Health) a distant, shrinking #2. Every share figure is an estimate from a journalist, consultancy or competitor, none audited.

### Cited Findings
- Market share estimates, oldest to newest:
  - 2016: Best Practice and MedicalDirector were about level at ~45% each when Affinity bought MD for $155m (March 2016) — [Medical Republic, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/best-practice-hints-at-a-transformational-plan/17474)
  - 2023: unnamed PHN survey data suggests BP holds more than 65% of the GP PMS market and MD about 25% or less — [Medical Republic, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/best-practice-hints-at-a-transformational-plan/17474)
  - 2026, ~60%: "estimated at around 60% of GP practices nationally". This is competitor (Halaxy) marketing with no source given — [Halaxy blog, 28 Apr 2026](https://blog.halaxy.com/best-practice-vs-medical-director-vs-halaxy-why-australian-gps-are-choosing-a-new-foundation-for-their-practice/). A consultancy also says ~60%, undated — [HCPA](https://www.hcpassociation.com.au/gp/best-practice-software/)
  - 2026, 65–70%: Bp Premier is "used by an estimated 65% to 70% of GP clinics" — [Medical Republic, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/best-practice-fee-hike-higher-for-part-timers/126739)
  - 2026, ~80%: "approximately 80% Best Practice, 15% Medical Director, and 5% everything else". The author, Jeremy Knibbs, says he is a non-executive director of competitor MediRecords — [Medical Republic opinion, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- Ownership and financials: Sonic Healthcare owns 49% of BP and the Pyefinch family 51%. BP's FY2025 consolidated revenue was $50.07m. BP invested $3.378m in AI scribe company Lyrebird Health. Sonic also owns 51% of "Cubico", a GP booking platform (the article's spelling; likely Cubiko or a related business, unverified) — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- Telstra Health acquired MedicalDirector for $340m. MD supported ~23,000 medical practitioners at acquisition (2019 figure, dated) — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212); [Telstra Exchange (2019)](https://www.telstra.com.au/exchange/telstra-health-acquires-medicaldirector-for-the-digital-future-of-health-care)
- BP says it serves 6,000+ clinics across Australia and New Zealand (vendor claim, undated in the search snippet) — [bpsoftware.net](https://bpsoftware.net)
- The ecosystem lock-in: BP earns substantial revenue from integration partners that pay for access through Halo Connect — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212). BP invested in Halo Connect, a FHIR interoperability platform built by the Cubiko developers — [Pulse+IT tag page, item dated 22 Aug 2022 (older)](https://www.pulseit.news/tag/bp-premier/)
- Other competitors named in sources:
  - MediRecords markets itself as cloud-native. It launched the MediRecords Evolve AI suite in Feb 2025 — [MediRecords, 28 Feb 2025](https://medirecords.com/tag/ai-patient-summary/)
  - Halaxy offers free core software with no per-practitioner fees and positions itself against BP and MD — [Halaxy blog, 28 Apr 2026](https://blog.halaxy.com/best-practice-vs-medical-director-vs-halaxy-why-australian-gps-are-choosing-a-new-foundation-for-their-practice/)
  - Magentus (Genie, Gentu; specialist market) appointed former Xero CCO Rachael Powell as CEO in Jul 2024 and chose Google Cloud for generative AI in Jun 2024 — [Pulse+IT Magentus tag](https://www.pulseit.news/tag/magentus/page/3/)
  - Medtech Evolution (NZ) has only 2014–2016 coverage, which is old — [Scoop, 2016](https://scoop.co.nz/stories/GE1607/S00024/medtech-evolution-gets-the-green-light.htm)

### Inferences
- A conservative, defensible framing is that BP holds well over half of Australian GP practices, probably 65–70% (the newest figure from a news report rather than an opinion piece), and MD roughly 15–25%. Growth from here is less about taking GP share and more about retention, revenue per practice (add-ons, AI, cloud modules), allied health and specialists, and NZ.
- Because BP is the incumbent, a cloud-native challenger could chip away at the edges, particularly with new or greenfield clinics and corporates, even without a big swing in share.

### Gaps
- No audited or Department of Health market share data. No 2025–26 figures for Medtech (NZ), Zedmed, Clinic to Cloud, Communicare or Genie share. Nothing found on whether Halo/Zedmed ownership changed recently.

## Competitor and company moves 2024–2026

### Takeaway
BP's own cloud move is slow: Bp Omni has been in beta in NZ since 2023, and the "Journey to the Cloud" mobile app slipped from 2025 to 2026. Bp Premier remains an on-premise SQL Server product. Telstra Health/MedicalDirector is pushing Helix cloud, AI scribing and eRequesting. Cloud-native challengers (MediRecords, Halaxy) market directly against BP's server burden.

### Cited Findings
- Bp Omni (formerly Project Titanium) launched as a SaaS beta for allied health in New Zealand. BP gave no Australian GP cloud date; the journalist estimated 2025 — [Medical Republic, 3 Jul 2023 (older)](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/best-practice-hints-at-a-transformational-plan/17474)
- BP announced its "Journey to the Cloud" strategy on 8 May 2025 — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/tag/bp-premier/). The steps, per vendor pages:
  - Best Practice Mobile first, then cloud-enabled modules (online smart forms, templates, digital payments), then cloud data hosting
  - An earlier vendor page said Mobile would launch in 2025; a later page says "coming 2026"
  - Mobile covers the appointment book, patient notes and photo upload off-site, on iOS and Android first, with a web version to follow
  - [bestpracticesoftware.com](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/?p=146536); [bestpracticesoftware.com](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/?p=146479)
- A FHIR-based GP mobile app was in beta in May 2024 — [Pulse+IT, 13 May 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/tag/bp-premier/)
- Third-party hosts such as PIP already run Bp Premier on private cloud. It is not a BP-branded product and depends on the practice's internet connection — [PIP](https://pip.com.au/medical-it/medical-cloud-hosting/best-practice/)
- A BP executive is quoted: "We have on-premise product that will change soon. Yes, we're not perfect." Medical Republic attributes the quote to BP's CTO and describes the architecture as early-2000s desktop/server. It contrasts BP's ~$1m Assignment of Benefit build with ~$50k at MediRecords — [Medical Republic opinion, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- BP's AI scribe moves:
  - Lyrebird integration announced 6 Feb 2024
  - Orchid SP2 with Lyrebird Scribe released 28 May 2024
  - Spectra release with an improved scribe and eScripts, 16 Oct 2024
  - Free Lyrebird tier for all Bp Premier customers, 16 Feb 2026
  - Sources: [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/tag/bp-premier/); [Best Practice blog](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/lyrebird-free-now-available-for-bp-premier-customers/); [Medical Republic](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/bp-premier-gives-game-changing-free-access-to-lyrebird-scribe/123517)
- Other BP launches: Best Health Booking online bookings (25 Mar 2024); Medcast CPD partnership (7 Aug 2025) — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/tag/bp-premier/)
- Competing AI scribe Heidi reportedly has only superficial access to BP records while BP builds a deeper Lyrebird integration. The column flags a risk of "shadow IT" scribes fragmenting data — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- Telstra Health/MedicalDirector moves:
  - Smart Scribe AI added to MD Clinical, Feb 2025 — [MedicalDirector](https://www.medicaldirector.com/press/telstra-health-launches-ai-powered-smart-scribe-in-medical-director-clinical)
  - Consultmed eReferrals embedded at no extra cost, Jul 2025 — [Telstra Health](https://www.telstrahealth.com/telstra-health-partners-with-consultmed/)
  - Smart Connect pathology eRequesting (with Healius) announced 17 Mar 2026 — [Telstra Health](https://www.telstrahealth.com/sectors/primary-community/); [Hospital + Healthcare](https://www.hospitalhealth.com.au/content/technology/product/telstra-health-smart-connect-pathology-erequesting-tool-1076063497)
  - Helix is marketed as server-free with automatic upgrades — [Telstra Health Helix](https://www.telstrahealth.com/products/helix/)
  - Telstra Health says it is building a new fully cloud and AI-based MD platform — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)

### Inferences
- The threat is less a single rival than three things together:
  - a credible cloud-native MD replacement
  - free or cheap cloud entrants (Halaxy) aimed at new clinics
  - AI scribes (Heidi) that capture the clinician's workflow outside the PMS
- BP's countermoves are the free Lyrebird tier, Halo Connect as the integration toll-road, and staged cloud modules.

### Gaps
- What "Journey to the Cloud" (8 May 2025) contains beyond vendor pages; whether Best Practice Mobile actually shipped in 2026. No 2024–26 acquisitions by BP were found.

## Customer pain points and sentiment

### Takeaway
I found few independent reviews. The documented pain points are:
- the on-premise server/IT burden and manual update cycles
- update-related bugs (notes not saving, HALO Link disconnects)
- a July 2026 price rise that hit part-timers hardest
- an unverified 2026 security vulnerability claim
- inertia: practices tolerate "good enough" software because switching is costly

### Cited Findings
- Price rise effective 1 Jul 2026:
  - Full-time licence: $1,489.41/yr, up 5% from $1,418.49
  - Part-time licence: $893.65/yr, up from $709.24 (+$184.41); part-time now costs 60% of the full fee instead of 50%
  - Prices were last updated in July 2024. The article notes that part-time GPs are more likely to be female
  - BP said the change "reflects both the standard 5% uplift and a structural adjustment to align part-time pricing with actual system usage"
  - [Medical Republic, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/best-practice-fee-hike-higher-for-part-timers/126739)
- GP Dr Max Mollenkopf describes a "Stockholm syndrome" where practices put up with a "good enough" product. Practice owners carry the cost and risk of migrating while GPs get the benefit — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- Competitor critique (biased): on-premise servers, hardware refresh cycles, IT support contracts, "quarterly or even yearly manual software updates that disrupt workflow", AI as a separate-login add-on, and a limited patient portal — [Halaxy, 28 Apr 2026](https://blog.halaxy.com/best-practice-vs-medical-director-vs-halaxy-why-australian-gps-are-choosing-a-new-foundation-for-their-practice/)
- Known issues published by BP:
  - After the Oxford SP1 update, some sites lost the server–client connection and the HALO Link connection to third-party integrations
  - Tx Control errors in the Inbox were fixed in build 1.13.0.1061
  - [BP KB known issues, undated](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/AllKnownIssues.htm)
  - Spectra/Spectra SP1 had clinical notes not saving, linked to network disruption and sleep/hibernate settings — [BP blog (approximately 2025, date not confirmed)](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/an-update-for-users-of-bp-premier-spectra-spectra-sp1/)
  - When the whole site goes down, the fix is usually restarting the SQL instance on the practice server, which illustrates the on-premise burden — [BP KB](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/spectra/Troubleshooting/PremierExits.htm)
- UNVERIFIED: a dev.to post claims CVE-2026-114119 was published 21 Oct 2026. It describes a hardcoded SQL Server admin password in Bp Premier builds before 1.14.1.1128. This is a single non-authoritative source; check the CVE record or BP before citing — [dev.to](https://dev.to/kozhevniko/a-password-you-cannot-change-cve-2026-114119-and-the-hardcoded-sql-server-administrator-in-bp-2037)
- Reader comments in Medical Republic say Helix (MD's cloud product) fails to send referrals properly to Queensland public hospitals. This is anecdotal and points to competitor weakness too — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- SoftwareReview lists no written reviews for Bp Premier — [SoftwareReview](https://softwarereview.com/s/bp-premier)

### Inferences
- Retention looks driven by switching costs more than delight, so churn risk rises when a credible cloud alternative lowers the migration cost.
- Areas a PM could own: price-fairness perception (part-timers), update reliability and release quality, reducing the server/IT burden, and making BP the AI scribe hub so data stays in the record.

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/AusDoctors), Capterra, ProductReview or newsGP user sentiment surfaced. No dated major outage was reported in the press for 2024–26. No NPS or churn data.

## Regulatory drivers

### Takeaway
Software must keep pace with:
- Share by Default for pathology and imaging reports (from 1 Jul 2026)
- eRequesting rollout
- Assignment of Benefit changes
- My Health Record conformance
- AI scribe medicolegal guidance
- cyber expectations heightened by the 2024 MediSecure breach

### Cited Findings
- Share by Default: pathology and diagnostic imaging reports must be uploaded to My Health Record from 1 Jul 2026 unless an exception or extension applies. Patients can still ask that reports not be shared. Providers need conformant software listed on the Conformance Register — [Dept of Health](https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/modernising-my-health-record-improved-access-to-health-information); [SWSPHN, 22 Jun 2026](https://swsphn.com.au/news/share-by-default-changes-to-my-health-record-from-1-july-2026/); [legislation F2025L01568](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01568/asmade/text)
- Uploads of these reports rose 105% after the start, to over 5 million a week — [ADHA release, 8 Jul 2026](https://www.digitalhealth.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/australians-already-seeing-the-benefits-of-sharing-health-information-by-default-08-07-2026.pdf)
- eRequesting in BP:
  - Needs Lava SP3 or later, with the HPI-O and HPI-I recorded in the software — [Brisbane South PHN fact sheet](https://bsphn.org.au/documents/erequesting-fact-sheet.pdf)
  - BP users must have eOrders activated before sending eRequests — [Clinical Labs](https://clinicallabs.com.au/doctor/erequests)
  - MD launched Smart Connect eRequesting in Mar 2026 (see above)
- Assignment of Benefit: BP's build reportedly cost ~$1m — [Medical Republic, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- MediSecure breach:
  - Detected 13 Apr 2024 and announced 16 May 2024
  - About 12.9 million Australians affected, ~6.5TB of data from prescriptions handled Mar 2019–Nov 2023
  - The company went into voluntary administration
  - [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/medisecure-data-breach-affects-about-129-million-australians-609924); [Information Age (ACS), Jul 2024](https://ia.acs.org.au/article/2024/medisecure-confirms-13m-australians-data-stolen-in-breach.html)
- AI scribes:
  - GP use of AI scribes rose from under 3% (May 2024) to 8.24% (Oct 2024)
  - Scribes that do not support diagnosis are generally exempt from TGA registration
  - GPs are advised to review indemnity clauses in vendor agreements
  - [RACGP medicolegal paper](https://www1.racgp.org.au/getattachment/bc6aedb7-2213-40bd-8e34-09d26edd5fc0/Is-AI-A-OK-Medicolegal-considerations-for-general.aspx)

### Inferences
- Each mandate brings conformance work that is costly on a legacy desktop codebase (see the AoB cost comparison). This is both a cost and a moat: smaller vendors struggle to keep up, and BP's scale amortises the work.

### Gaps
- Current TGA AI scribe guidance was not fetched. No found link between MyMedicare and PMS requirements. No BP-specific Share by Default conformance notice was found.

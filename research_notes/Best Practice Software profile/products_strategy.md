# Best Practice Software (Bp): Product Line and Product Strategy (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 29 Oct 2026. Many Pulse+IT articles are paywalled after the intro, so only the visible lede was usable. Items before 2024 are flagged [OLDER].

## Product line: what Bp sells and the status of each

### Takeaway
Bp's revenue base is still Bp Premier, its on-premise Australian GP practice management software (PMS). Release names change through the year (Orchid 2024, Spectra Oct 2024, Oxford June 2026). Around it sit patient-facing add-ons: Bp Comms/Bp SMS, the Best Health app, Best Health Booking (2024) and Best Practice Mobile (2024–25). Bp Omni, the cloud product, is still limited to NZ allied health. Bp no longer gives it prominence on its main website.

### Cited Findings
- **Bp Premier**: the flagship. Bp describes it as "Australia's leading software for clinical treatment, front office staff, practice, and financial management". The site lists GP, Allied Health, Mixed, Specialist and Enterprise clinic types, an "Australia-based" support team and "over 20 years" with Australian providers. Site viewed Oct 2026 — [bestpracticesoftware.com](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/bp-omni/) (this URL now redirects to the general product page)
- **Current Bp Premier version: Oxford 1.14.0.1114, released 9 June 2026.** It requires the April 2026 Data Update, and some changes are switched off until 1 July 2026. Oxford SP1 (1.14.0.1118) was still in beta/Industry Preview with release date "TBC" (notes updated 3 July 2026) — [Oxford release notes](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/BpPremier_Oxford_ReleaseNotes.htm); [Oxford SP1 beta notes](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/BpPremier_OxfordSP1_ReleaseNotes.htm)
- **Oxford features** (blog, 21 May 2026). Immunisation updates (nasal route, Prevenar 20, revised eligibility). MySL "Date Created" for ADHA compliance. Automatic smoking pack-years. Bulk-billing voucher window: from 5 Sep 2026, batches include only vouchers from the last 12 months. Chronic Condition Management and LARC claiming rule updates. Copy function for authority scripts. eHealth certificate import tool that also updates the eRx gateway certificate. **Digital SMS Assignment of Benefits from 1 July 2026**, linked to the SMS Sender ID Register — [Bp blog: What's new in Bp Premier Oxford](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/whats-new-in-bp-premier-oxford/)

- **Best Health Booking (BHB)**: online booking, launched 25 Mar 2024. Patients and practice teams both see the Bp Premier appointment book in real time. ### Inferences
- Bp's product-led growth in 2024–26 has come from add-on modules around Premier (booking, mobile, SMS benefits assignment, AI scribe) and not from Omni. Omni's absence from the current website suggests the cloud effort shifted from "new product" to "incrementally cloud-enable Premier". This is inference; Bp has made no statement about it.

### Gaps
- No public launch or status details found for "Bp Patient" as a distinct product. The patient app is branded "Best Health".
- No current (2024–26) status for Bp Allied, the NZ build of Bp Premier, or Bp VIP.net.
- No 2024–26 NZ market-share data and no Medtech comparison found.
- No current customer, practice or user counts found.

## Cloud migration: Bp Omni and "Journey to the Cloud"

### Takeaway
In May 2025 Bp replaced its "move to Omni" story with **"Journey to the Cloud"**, an opt-in, step-by-step plan to cloud-enable Bp Premier ("No forced migrations, no loss of control"). The sequence runs from Best Practice Mobile to cloud modules (smart forms, templates, digital payments) to BpID single sign-on to cloud hosting and linked practices, and only then toward Omni. No migration numbers have been published.

### Cited Findings
- Pulse+IT, 8 May 2025: Bp "unveiled an ambitious new cloud strategy" called Journey to the Cloud. It adds cloud-based features, stronger data security and remote access to Bp Premier — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-software-announces-new-journey-to-the-cloud/)
- Bp's own page, 24 Jun 2025, opens with "a step-by-step transformation into a powerful, cloud-enabled platform". It is opt-in and incremental, with "No forced migrations, no loss of control". It lists cloud modules expected "this year" (2025): online smart forms, templates and digital payments. Cloud data hosting is planned in a unified environment meeting "new regulatory standards" — [Bp: Welcome to our Journey to the Cloud](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/?p=146536)
- Roadmap content indexed from Bp (search snippet, c. 2025) gives a **2025–2028** timeline: Best Practice Mobile, then Bp Premier cloud modules, then BpID (single sign-on) for Premier, then hosting and linked practice environments, then Premier modules moving toward Bp Omni. It targets Australian GP practices on Premier first, with allied health and specialists later — [Bp Journey to the Cloud](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/?p=146536); [Bp Summit plenary "Journey to the Cloud" PDF (now 404)](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/BpSummit/Presentations/PDFs/28.%20Plenary%20D%20-%20Journey%20to%20the%20Cloud.pdf)
- History [OLDER]:
  - 2017: Bp was working on a cloud version nicknamed "Titanium" — [Pulse+IT, 27 Jan 2017](https://www.pulseit.news/new-zealand-digital-health/best-practice-eyes-movement-in-the-new-zealand-pms-market/)
  - 2023: Omni (Titanium) beta in NZ allied health after six or more years of development. **Halo Connect**, a FHIR-based API platform in which Bp is a major shareholder, was positioned to help integrations and Premier customers move to the cloud. CPO **Danielle Bancroft** confirmed Omni was "the first step" (paraphrase). Medical Republic's own *estimate* (not a Bp commitment) was an AU release by 2025 — [Medical Republic, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/best-practice-hints-at-a-transformational-plan/17474)
- Third-party option: PIP and other IT providers offer private-cloud hosting of on-prem Bp Premier. PIP says this is not a Bp-branded SaaS product — [PIP](https://pip.com.au/medical-it/medical-cloud-hosting/best-practice/)

### Inferences
- The 2025 reset puts continuity first for a >65% installed base: cloud value is added inside Premier, not through a disruptive move to a new product. For a PM, the core problems are hybrid architecture (on-prem database plus cloud modules), identity (BpID), and incremental opt-in adoption measurement.
- Bp missed the 2025 AU cloud date that Medical Republic estimated in 2023. Omni has not reached AU GPs as of Oct 2026, based on the sources found.

### Gaps
- No public figures on how many practices use Best Practice Mobile, the cloud modules, or Omni.
- No reported migration pain points found in trade press (Medical Republic, Australian Doctor and newsGP searches returned nothing relevant).
- Could not confirm which 2025 cloud modules (smart forms, digital payments) actually shipped, or whether BpID is live.

## Patient engagement, payments, eScripts, MHR, AI scribes

### Takeaway
Patient engagement is Bp Comms/SMS plus Best Health app plus Best Health Booking. Payments go through partners (Tyro first, plus HICAPS and CommBank Smart Health); "digital payments" was named as a 2025 cloud module. AI scribing is partner-led, mainly with Lyrebird (integrated 2024; a free tier for Premier customers since Feb 2026) and Heidi (third-party integration). In Sept 2026 Bp moved into clinical AI decision support by leading Medcast's raise (MedLuma).

### Cited Findings
- **Payments**:
  - Tyro calls itself Bp's "first and longest-standing integrated payments partner". It covers Bp Allied, Premier and VIP.net, with EFTPOS and credit card started from a Premier invoice and Medicare Easyclaim direct billing — [Bp: Tyro Health partner page](https://bpsoftware.net/partner-tyro); [Bp KB: Tyro direct billing](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/PaymentIntegrations/Tyro/ConfigureTyroDirectBilling.htm)
  - The Bp Payments Utility supports **HICAPS, Tyro and CommBank Smart Health** — [Bp KB: Payment Integrations (Spectra)](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/spectra/PaymentIntegrations/PaymentIntegrations.htm)
  - Tyro acquired Medipass (A$22.5m, per Startup Daily) and later rebranded it Tyro Health Online — [Pulse+IT Tyro tag](https://pulseit.news/tag/tyro); [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/?p=89047) [OLDER]
  - "Digital payments" was listed as a 2025 Premier cloud module — [Bp Journey to the Cloud, 24 Jun 2025](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/?p=146536)
- **SMS Assignment of Benefits** (patients assign Medicare benefits digitally by SMS) starts 1 July 2026 in Oxford — [Bp blog, 21 May 2026](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/whats-new-in-bp-premier-oxford/)
- **eScripts**: Spectra (Oct 2024) improved eScripts. Oxford adds MySL Date Created and the eRx certificate update — [Pulse+IT, 16 Oct 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-releases-spectra-version-featuring-improved-ai-scribe-and-escripts-capability/); [Bp blog, 21 May 2026](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/whats-new-in-bp-premier-oxford/)
- **MyMedicare**: bulk import tool for registered patient lists, 21 Feb 2024 — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-releases-mymedicare-bulk-import-tool-for-registered-patient-lists/)
- **AI scribes**:
  - **Lyrebird** integration in Orchid SP2 (28 May 2024), improved in Spectra (16 Oct 2024) — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-releases-big-update-to-orchid-including-lyrebird-scribe-integration/); [Lyrebird blog](https://lyrebirdhealth.com/au/blogs/new-integration-best-practice-software)
  - **16 Feb 2026: Bp launched a free tier of Lyrebird's AI scribe for Bp Premier customers.** Bp's framing: "Administrative burden on general practitioners has increased significantly in recent years". Tier limits were not visible (paywall) — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-launches-free-lyrebird-ai-scribe-tier/)
  - **Heidi Health** integrates with Bp Premier through Bp's third-party integrations setup. The appointment book syncs to Heidi sessions, and notes go back to the Bp record. Heidi's pages conflict on which tier is required (Practice/Enterprise vs Together/Enterprise) — [Heidi: Best Practice integration](https://www.heidihealth.com/en-au/integrations/best-practice); [Heidi support](https://support.heidihealth.com/en/articles/9382109-best-practice)
- **Clinical AI**: 21 Sep 2026, Bp led a **$5.4m raise in Medcast** to speed up MedLuma, an AI tool that gives guideline-grounded, cited answers. MedLuma launched in early 2026, keeps data onshore, and will be integrated into Bp software — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-leads-5-4m-investment-in-clinical-ai/)

### Inferences
- Bp builds AI capability through partners and investment rather than in-house. The free Lyrebird tier looks like a defensive move against standalone scribes such as Heidi, keeping scribing inside the Bp ecosystem. The second point is inference.

### Gaps
- No Bp-native telehealth product found in 2024–26 sources.
- No 2024–26 Bp-specific My Health Record announcement retrieved. The Bp blog sidebar referenced an "October My Health Record article" (Oct 2026), but it was not fetched.
- No "Bp Payments" product beyond the Payments Utility, and no confirmation that the 2025 "digital payments" cloud module shipped.

## Partnerships and integrations, 2024 to 2026

### Takeaway
Bp runs a curated **Partner Network of over 130 third-party solutions** and adds partners steadily: analytics (Cubiko), AI scribe (Lyrebird, Heidi), CPD and clinical AI (Medcast), aged-care medication (BESTMED), and patient front door (HotHealth). It is also building sector goodwill through research partnerships, grants and scholarships.

## Leadership, vision and strategy quotes

### Takeaway
**Craig Hodges** has been CEO since June 2024, succeeding the founders Frank and Lorraine Pyefinch, who stayed on the board as chair and director. His public line on AI is that it "will always complement – not replace – the clinical judgment". The cloud vision is framed as opt-in and step by step: "No forced migrations, no loss of control".

### Cited Findings
- 14 Jun 2024: the founders appointed long-time deputy Craig Hodges as CEO. Frank Pyefinch became chair and Lorraine Pyefinch a board director — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/movers-and-shakers/best-practice-founders-hand-over-the-baton-to-new-ceo/); [Bp announcement](https://bpsoftware.net/?p=144299)
- Hodges on AI, 21 Sep 2026: AI "will always complement – not replace – the clinical judgment". On the Medcast deal: "Our lead investment in Medcast reflects our confidence in the team, the MedLuma platform". Bp's stated aim is to make clinical information easier to reach inside existing clinician workflows — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-leads-5-4m-investment-in-clinical-ai/)
- Cloud vision (Bp, 24 Jun 2025): "a step-by-step transformation into a powerful, cloud-enabled platform"; "No forced migrations, no loss of control" — [Bp Journey to the Cloud](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/?p=146536)
- On GP burden (Feb 2026): "Administrative burden on general practitioners has increased significantly in recent years" — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-launches-free-lyrebird-ai-scribe-tier/)
- [OLDER] Danielle Bancroft (then Chief Product Officer) described Omni as the first step toward a unified cloud platform for allied health, GPs and specialists (paraphrased, 2023) — [Medical Republic](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/best-practice-hints-at-a-transformational-plan/17474)
- Conflicting item: an undated Bundaberg Now piece says Craig Hodges left for a Queensland Health role, with Lorraine Pyefinch as acting COO — [Bundaberg Now](https://www.bundabergnow.com/?p=14822). This is likely an earlier departure before he returned, since Pulse+IT quotes him as CEO in Sep 2026. It is unverified.

### Inferences
- Product themes a PM application could mirror:
  - reduce GP admin burden (scribes, SMS benefits assignment, booking)
  - continuity and trust over disruption (opt-in cloud)
  - AI as a clinician aid with provenance (MedLuma)
  - ecosystem and partner breadth (130+ partners)

### Gaps

- Current CPO not confirmed. Danielle Bancroft's 2023 title may be out of date.

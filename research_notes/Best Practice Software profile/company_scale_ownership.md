# Best Practice Software (Bp): Scale, Ownership and Leadership (as of 29 Oct 2026)

Note: the current corporate site is bestpracticesoftware.com; bpsoftware.net still hosts the knowledge base and some legacy pages.

## 1. How many practices, clinicians, users and patients use Bp products, and what share of Australian GP practices?

### Takeaway
Bp's own product page (live Oct 2026) claims "over 7,000 practices and 27,000 medical professionals" use Bp Premier. Third-party market-share estimates range from about 65% of GP practices (Medical Republic, June 2024) to about 80% of the GP PMS market (Health Services Daily / Medical Republic opinion piece, Sept 2026). No patient count is published.

### Cited Findings
- Bp Premier page: "Over 7,000 practices and 27,000 medical professionals" trust Bp Premier; "over 30 Support Specialists"; "200+ trusted third-party integrations"; >1,000 knowledge-base articles. Page is (c) 2026 and carries a blog post dated 27 Oct 2026. (Vendor marketing figure.) — [Bp Premier, bestpracticesoftware.com, accessed Oct 2026](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/products/bp-premier/)
- The History & Values page gives a lower, probably older figure: "over 6,000 healthcare clinics across Australia and New Zealand". It gives no patient count. — [History & Values, accessed Oct 2026](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/about/history-values/)
- Market share, Sept 2026: Bp has about 80% of the GP patient management system market, MedicalDirector about 15% (elsewhere 15 to 20%), others about 5%. This is an opinion piece by Jeremy Knibbs, who discloses he is a non-executive director of competitor MediRecords. — [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729); also syndicated at [Medical Republic](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/129212)
- Market share, June 2024: Bp is "understood" to have about 65% of GP practices and about 70% of GPs. Bp plus MedicalDirector make up about 90% of the GP desktop market. — [Medical Republic, 20 Jun 2024](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/why-does-sonic-want-to-control-both-cubiko-and-best-practice/108495)
- Described as "Australia's largest provider of clinical and practice management software" (Sept 2026). — [Dealroom, 21 Sep 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/155154-best-practice-leads-3-9m-raise-for-australian-clinical-ai-startup-medcas/)
- Halo Connect, the integration layer Bp backs, was reported as available in "over 4000 practices" using Bp in Australia. Third-party integrations had to move to Halo Connect by 31 Dec 2025. — [The PHN notice](https://thephn.com.au/news/halo-connect-and-best-practice-action-required-before-31-december-2025); [Bp page on Halo Connect](https://bpsoftware.net/interoperability-halo-connect/)
- About 250 integration partners via Halo Connect (Sept 2026). — [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729)
- An HCPA consultancy page estimates about 60% share of Australian GP practices, with no method given (lower quality). — [HCPA](https://www.hcpassociation.com.au/gp/best-practice-software/)
- NZ: Bp entered the NZ market by buying Houston Medical (VIP.net) assets in April 2015 and adapted Bp Premier for NZ GPs from about 2016. Bp VIP.net enters maintenance mode on 31 Dec 2026, with technical support until 30 Jun 2027. — [Bp VIP.net sunset notice](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/bp-vip-net-sunset-nz/); [Pulse+IT, Houston Medical purchase](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-buys-nz-software-vendor-houston-medical/); [Medit (reseller)](https://medit.com.au/best-practice/)

### Inferences
- A safe slide line is "7,000+ practices and 27,000+ clinicians (company figure, 2026)" with "about two-thirds to four-fifths of Australian GP practices" for share. The 80% figure comes from a competitor-affiliated opinion writer. The 65%/70% figure is older (2024) and "understood", not confirmed.
- The 6,000 to 7,000 gap between the two company pages suggests growth, or that the product page counts all Bp Premier sites, including allied and specialist practices.

### Gaps
- No published patient count. No NZ-only practice or user count.
- No official date attached to the 7,000/27,000 figure, beyond the page being live in Oct 2026.

## 2. Staff numbers, offices, revenue and growth

### Takeaway
The headquarters and registered office are in Bundaberg, Qld (86 Woongarra St), with a Brisbane Business Centre (opened 2021), a Sydney Support Centre and a Hamilton NZ operations centre (opened 2016). Headcount is roughly 180 to 240 depending on source; there is no official figure. Reported FY2025 consolidated revenue was A$50.07m.

### Cited Findings
- Revenue: consolidated revenue of A$50.07 million in FY2025. — [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729)
- Offices and timeline: founded in Bundaberg in 2004; purpose-built Bundaberg HQ in 2015; Hamilton NZ operations centre opened 2016; Brisbane Business Centre opened 2021 "with a significantly expanded team"; 2025 new brand, website and strategy plus a refurbished Bundaberg registered office; the 2025 Bp Summit in Brisbane had 400 delegates. — [History & Values](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/about/history-values/)
- The About page lists the Brisbane Business Centre, Hamilton Operations Centre, Sydney Support Centre and Bundaberg Operations Hub. — [bpomni.co.nz About Us](https://bpomni.co.nz/about-us/)
- Staff, all third-party estimates:
  - "over 240 staff" across Bundaberg, Brisbane, Sydney and Hamilton (undated) — [Talking HealthTech profile](https://www.talkinghealthtech.com/best-practice-software)
  - about 217 employees (June 2026) — [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/best-practice-software/5a1d98e72300005300878163)
  - LinkedIn: size band 201 to 500, 184 on the employee tab — [LinkedIn](https://au.linkedin.com/company/best-practice-software)
  - about 155 — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/481261-15)
- Glassdoor: 3.2/5 over 21 reviews, 66% approve of the CEO. Some reviews mention exec churn (small sample). — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Best-Practice-Software-Reviews-E1390168.htm)

### Inferences
- "About 200 staff" is a defensible rounded figure, but it should be labelled as an estimate.
- A$50m revenue across about 7,000 practices works out to roughly A$7k per practice per year. This is my own arithmetic, not a sourced figure.

### Gaps
- No official headcount, revenue growth rate or profit figures. FY2024 and FY2026 revenue were not found.
- The FY2025 revenue figure has a single source; the underlying filing (presumably ASIC or a Sonic disclosure) was not viewed.

## 3. Ownership history and investors

### Takeaway
Bp is founder-controlled and not private-equity owned. The Pyefinch family holds 51% and ASX-listed Sonic Healthcare holds 49% (reported 2024 and 2026). I found no evidence of any Pacific Equity Partners, Dedalus or other PE involvement. A large corporate reportedly made an uncompleted offer "a few years" before 2024.

### Cited Findings
- Founded in Bundaberg in 2004 by GP Dr Frank Pyefinch, after he exited his earlier software business (MedicalDirector) in 2002. He had reportedly built that software to about 90% of Australian GP desktops. — [History & Values](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/about/history-values/); [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729)
- Ownership: Pyefinch family 51%, Sonic Healthcare 49% (Sept 2026). — [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729); [Dealroom, 21 Sep 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/155154-best-practice-leads-3-9m-raise-for-australian-clinical-ai-startup-medcas/)
- June 2024 details:
  - Sonic owns 49% but does not control Bp.
  - Sonic CFO Christopher Wilks is one of only two Bp directors.
  - Lorraine Pyefinch holds the remaining shares and Frank Pyefinch is not on the register.
  - The article questions whether Sonic wants control of both Cubiko and Bp.
  - "A large corporate" made an unnamed high offer a few years earlier that did not complete.
  - The article is internally inconsistent on the control margin.
  - [Medical Republic, 20 Jun 2024](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/why-does-sonic-want-to-control-both-cubiko-and-best-practice/108495)
- Founders moved to the board in June 2024: Dr Frank Pyefinch as chair, Lorraine Pyefinch as director. — [Pulse+IT, 14 Jun 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/movers-and-shakers/best-practice-founders-hand-over-the-baton-to-new-ceo/)
- Sonic is valued at about A$9.7bn (Sept 2026). — [Dealroom, 21 Sep 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/155154-best-practice-leads-3-9m-raise-for-australian-clinical-ai-startup-medcas/)

### Inferences
- The brief's "Pacific Equity Partners" premise looks wrong. No source links PEP or any PE fund to Bp, and the cap table reported in 2024 and 2026 is Pyefinch plus Sonic only. Treat any PE claim as unverified.
- The "Dedalus" premise is also unsupported. The PE and corporate churn in this market belongs to competitor MedicalDirector: HCN, then Primary Health Care, then Affinity Equity Partners (A$155m, 2016), then Telstra Health (A$340m, 2021). — [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729)

### Gaps
- When and at what price Sonic acquired its 49% was not found. Sonic's annual report notes were not reviewed.
- No reported valuation or formal sale process for Bp was found in AFR or The Australian (paywalled; not searched in depth).

## 4. Acquisitions, investments and partnerships

### Takeaway
Bp has made only two acquisitions, both pre-2016: Houston Medical/VIP.net (NZ, April 2015) and one other, unnamed in company materials, around 2013. Its recent activity is minority investments and partnerships: Halo Connect, Lyrebird Health (A$3.378m), Medcast/MedLuma (led a US$3.49m round, Sept 2026) and Cubiko-related ties through Sonic.

### Cited Findings
- Bp says it has acquired "two additional software companies" in Australia and NZ, extending into specialists and allied health. The timeline places acquisitions around 2013 (older). — [History & Values](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/about/history-values/); [About Us](https://bpomni.co.nz/about-us/)
- April 2015 (older): bought the assets and IP of Hamilton NZ vendor Houston Medical, maker of VIP.net, which is used mainly by specialists and allied health. It was later rebranded Bp VIP.net and is now being sunset (maintenance mode 31 Dec 2026). — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-buys-nz-software-vendor-houston-medical/); [Bp sunset page](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/bp-vip-net-sunset-nz/); [Xestro CEO blog, 30 Mar 2026](https://xestro.com/2026/03/30/from-our-ceo-the-end-of-an-era-farewell-to-bp-vip-net/)
- Halo Connect: undisclosed investment at launch (the platform was built by the Cubiko developers). Bp holds a stake in Halo Wing Pty Ltd. Halo Connect is now the mandatory integration route for Bp Premier (31 Dec 2025 deadline). — [Healthcare IT News](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/anz/cubiko-developers-launch-cloud-based-interoperability-platform-halo-connect); [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729); [The PHN](https://thephn.com.au/news/halo-connect-and-best-practice-action-required-before-31-december-2025)
- Lyrebird Health: partnership announced 6 Feb 2024 to integrate the AI scribe into Bp Premier. Lyrebird Free was made available to all Bp Premier customers on 16 Feb 2026. Bp invested A$3.378m in Lyrebird. — [Bp blog, Feb 2024](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/best-practice-and-lyrebird-health-partnership/); [Bp blog, Feb 2026](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/lyrebird-free-now-available-for-bp-premier-customers/); [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729)
- Medcast: Bp led a US$3.49m (about A$3.9m) raise for Medcast to scale MedLuma, its AI clinical knowledge platform (21 Sep 2026). — [Dealroom, 21 Sep 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/155154-best-practice-leads-3-9m-raise-for-australian-clinical-ai-startup-medcas/)

### Inferences
- The brief's premises "Bp Allied", "Medical Wizard" and "Clinic to Cloud" are not supported by any source found. Do not use them on the slide.
- Strategic direction since 2024 is ecosystem and AI through minority stakes and partnerships (Halo, Lyrebird, Medcast), not roll-up M&A.

### Gaps
- The second acquired company (circa 2013) was not identified. No price was found for Houston Medical.

## 5. Leadership

### Takeaway
Craig Hodges has been CEO since June 2024; founders Frank Pyefinch (chair) and Lorraine Pyefinch moved to the board. There is no titled CPO. Product sits under CTO Jessica White (product, platform, partnerships and technology), Simon Williams (Chief of Product Technology) and Joel Mortensen (Director, Product Design).

### Cited Findings
- CEO Craig Hodges (FCPA FAHRI GAICD), appointed June 2024. He was a long-time deputy who had served as General Manager, Chief Corporate & Legal Officer and COO. — [Pulse+IT, 14 Jun 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/movers-and-shakers/best-practice-founders-hand-over-the-baton-to-new-ceo/); [Bp blog: Introducing our new CEO](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/introducing-our-new-ceo/); [Leadership page](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/about/leadership/)
- Executive team on the Leadership page (accessed Oct 2026; no start dates given):
  - Cameron Cowell, COO
  - Jessica White, CTO: leads "product, platform, partnerships and technology"; joined Bp in 2015 and was Manager Commercial & Customer Enablement in 2019
  - David Woodard, CFO
  - Blake Hilgers, Chief of Customer Experience
  - Simon Williams, Chief of Product Technology
  - Alan Goodare, Chief of People Experience
  - Joel Mortensen, Director, Product Design: roadmaps, the product design lab, and sector analysis
  - Bec Bland, Director, Partnerships
  - Sean Finn, Director, Security & Infrastructure
  - [Leadership](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/about/leadership/); [Bp blog 2019 on Jessica White](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/bps-jessica-white-features-on-national-panel/)
- Jessica White was cited as CTO in Sept 2026. — [Health Services Daily, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.healthservicesdaily.com.au/is-the-dominance-of-best-practice-a-good-or-a-bad-thing/52729)
- Board (2024): Christopher Wilks (Sonic CFO) was one of two directors; Frank Pyefinch was chair per Pulse+IT. — [Medical Republic, 20 Jun 2024](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/why-does-sonic-want-to-control-both-cubiko-and-best-practice/108495); [Pulse+IT, 14 Jun 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/movers-and-shakers/best-practice-founders-hand-over-the-baton-to-new-ceo/)

### Inferences
- For a product or design role, the relevant chain is CTO Jessica White, then Simon Williams for product technology and Joel Mortensen for product design.
- The two board accounts conflict. Medical Republic names only two directors, including Wilks, while Pulse+IT names both Pyefinches on the board. The board composition may have changed around June 2024.

### Gaps
- No appointment dates for Jessica White as CTO, or for Cowell, Woodard or Williams.
- No 2025 or 2026 executive-change announcements were found. Glassdoor anecdotes mention exec churn but name no one.

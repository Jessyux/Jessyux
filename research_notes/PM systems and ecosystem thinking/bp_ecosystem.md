# Bp Premier as a system: actors, data flows and failure points (research notes, compiled 29 Oct 2026)

Scope note: Australia focus. Sources are mainly Best Practice (Bp) knowledge base (KB) pages, Pulse+IT, Medical Republic, Halo Connect and partner support pages. Items older than 2024 are flagged [older]. Bp KB pages exist on two domains (kb.bpsoftware.net and kb.bestpracticesoftware.com) and per-version folders (Saffron, Orchid, Spectra, Oxford); the version names show release history: Jade -> Saffron -> Orchid -> Spectra (Dec 2024) -> Oxford (Jun 2026) -> Oxford SP1 (Aug 2026).

## 1. Inside the practice: roles and modules

### Takeaway
Bp Premier is the shared work surface for front desk (appointments, invoicing, claiming, payment terminals), clinicians (clinical record, prescribing, pathology and imaging requests, referrals, inbox for results) and practice managers (configuration, MyMedicare site setup, reporting, third-party integration approval). Most role detail comes from KB structure, not from a single "roles" page.

### Cited Findings
- Clinicians request pathology from the patient record (Request > Pathology) and can attach a follow-up action; eOrders depend on a third-party secure messaging provider installed on the server or workstations, and labs added as contacts with a NATA code (page last updated 8 May 2025) — [Bp KB: Set up pathology and imaging](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Clinical/PathologyandRadiology/SetupPathology.htm); [Bp KB: Request pathology](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Clinical/PathologyandRadiology/RequestPathology.htm)
- Deleting an eOrder in Bp Premier does not cancel it with the lab; staff must contact the provider directly (a cross-system consistency gap) — [Bp KB: Request pathology](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Clinical/PathologyandRadiology/RequestPathology.htm)
- Guidance Based Requesting (pathology only) requires Spectra SP2+, a messaging provider and eOrdering set up (article dated 25 Feb 2025) — [Bp KB: Enable GBR](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Spectra/Integrations/GBR/EnableGBR.htm)
- Clinician inbox handles incoming pathology and imaging results — [Bp KB: Inbox, Pathology and Imaging tutorials](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Clinical/DoctorInboxLP.htm)
- Reception/billing: payment terminal integrations (HICAPS, Tyro, CommBank Smart Health) and Medicare Easyclaim run from the Bp Premier payment screen; Bp has a dedicated reception video library — [Bp KB: Payment Integrations](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/spectra/PaymentIntegrations/PaymentIntegrations.htm); [Bp KB: Reception videos](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Library/ReceptionVideos.htm)
- MyMedicare (Orchid SP1 Rev 1, released 8 Jan 2024): a "Registered for MyMedicare" flag and registration date on the patient; status shows in the appointment book, on invoices and in the clinical record demographics; a MyMedicare Site ID per location lets Bp apply the right incentive item numbers on invoices. This single field touches reception, billing and clinicians — [Bp KB: Orchid SP1 R1 release notes](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/WhatsNew/BpPremier_OrchidSP1R1_ReleaseNotes.htm)
- Practice managers/admins: approve third-party integrations at Setup > Configuration > Database > Set up Third-Party Integrations — [Bp KB: Partner Network (updated 30 Aug 2024)](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/Management/PartnerNetwork.htm)
- Oxford SP1 (19 Aug 2026) added a Database Password Access Manager where administrators approve workstations, plus updates to the Reporting Tool, Payments Utility and NCSR (National Cancer Screening Register) Utility — [Bp KB: What's new in Bp Premier (Oxford)](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm)
- Bp publishes clinical-programme guidance inside the product, e.g. setting up the new Medicare Healthy Kids Check (from 1 Nov 2026) in Bp Premier (blog 27 Oct 2026) — [Bp blog](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/preparing-for-the-medicare-healthy-kids-check-tools-and-resources-in-bp-premier/)
- Bp Premier has a separate Bp Premier Reporting Tool with its own release notes — [Bp KB: Reporting Tool release notes](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/WhatsNew/RNBpReportingTool.htm)
- Bp customer scale: Medical Republic reports Bp educated "around 5,000 customers" on AoB and that 1,500 clinics had already moved to the June 2026 Oxford build (22 Jun 2026) — [Medical Republic](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/?p=126623)

### Inferences
- A single regulatory field (MyMedicare registration, enduring AoB flag) flows through appointment book -> invoice -> claim -> incentive payment, so changes to it are cross-role changes, not "billing features".
- Pathology eOrdering depends on three parties outside Bp (messaging vendor, lab, and the local Windows machine config), which is why troubleshooting lives at the practice server level.

### Gaps
- No single Bp page found that maps roles to permissions; role-to-module mapping above is inferred from KB structure.
- Did not verify recall/SMS reminder module details (Bp's own SMS vs partners like HotDoc) in this pass.

## 2. Government and national systems

### Takeaway
Bp Premier is the conduit between the practice and national infrastructure: Medicare claiming (via Easyclaim/online claiming and terminals), AoB rules, MyMedicare, My Health Record (conformance-registered), electronic prescribing (MySL/Active Script List), the AIR, NCSR, and eRequesting. Each government change lands as a Bp release, often under tight dates.

### Cited Findings
- Assignment of Benefit (AoB) reform: Oxford (released 9 Jun 2026) added AoB forms sent by SMS that patients complete on their own devices, built for a 1 July 2026 go-live. The release was then pulled ("no longer available to download") after the Government/Department of Health, Disability and Ageing announced changes on 18 June 2026 — [Bp KB: What's new (Oxford)](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm); [Bp support notice on AoB changes](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/support/AoBchanges.htm)
- Amended AoB rules: verbal consent accepted in all settings for 12 months from 1 July 2026; enduring AoB brought forward to 1 July 2026 for MyMedicare-registered patients, aged care residents and ACCHO patients (previously planned April 2027 per Bp) — [SEMPHN](https://www.semphn.org.au/updates-to-the-assignment-of-benefit-process); [Dept of Health AoB FAQ (Aug 2026, docx)](https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-08/assignment-of-medicare-benefits-for-bulk-billing-frequently-asked-questions.docx); [RACGP newsGP](https://www1.racgp.org.au/newsgp/professional/commonsense-delay-to-assignment-of-benefit-changes); [Bp KB Oxford SP1 notes](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm)
- Oxford SP1 (19 Aug 2026): AoB enabled under Setup > Configuration > Online Claiming (cannot be disabled once enabled); "Enduring AoB" checkbox added to patient details — [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm)
- Bulk bill claim cut-off change: from 5 Sep 2025, Medicare won't accept bulk bill claims older than 1 year for services on/after that date (2 years before) — reflected in the Oxford release — [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm)
- Medicare Easyclaim runs through terminals: HICAPS (integrated Easyclaim, or Medicare Online paying rebates to patient bank account), Tyro (Integrated Medicare Easyclaim), CommBank Smart Health (Albert terminal only, fully paid accounts only; practices must pick Tyro OR Smart Health for Easyclaim, not both; requires Bp Payments Utility on server and every workstation plus a CommBank API key) — [Bp KB: Payment Integrations](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/spectra/PaymentIntegrations/PaymentIntegrations.htm); [Bp KB: CommBank Smart Health](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/spectra/PaymentIntegrations/CommBankSmartHealth/CommBankSmartHealth.htm); [Bp KB: Tyro](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/saffron/ThirdPartyInterfaces/Tyro/WhatClaimTyro.htm); [Bp KB: HICAPS Easyclaim](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/PaymentIntegrations/HICAPS/EasyClaimPayWithHICAPS.htm)
- MyMedicare: registration flag, Site ID and auto incentive items (8 Jan 2024); MyMedicare Import Tool needs Orchid SP1 R1 + Dec 2023 data update — [Bp KB Orchid SP1 R1](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/WhatsNew/BpPremier_OrchidSP1R1_ReleaseNotes.htm)
- My Health Record: Bp Premier 1.12.1 listed on the ADHA MHR B2B conformance register dated 7 Jan 2026 — [ADHA conformance register](https://www.digitalhealth.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/mhr-b2b-conformance-register-20260107.pdf). Shared Health Summary upload from Bp marks "Send to My Health Record" — [Bp KB: Create a Shared Health Summary](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Integrations/MyHealthRecord/CreatingaSharedHealthSummary.htm)
- Share by Default: legislative requirements take effect 1 July 2026 for pathology and diagnostic imaging reports (generally uploaded to MHR within 24 hours of release to the requester); prescribing/dispensing data was under consultation — [ADHA Partnership Pulse ed. 7](https://www.digitalhealth.gov.au/newsroom/partnership-pulse-newsletter/partnership-pulse-edition-7)
- My Health Record Rules 2026: Bp blog on practice policy and recordkeeping updates (27 Oct 2026) — [Bp blog](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/blog/my-health-record-access-practice-policy-and-recordkeeping-updates-in-october/)
- ePrescribing: Orchid SP1 (Jun 2023) added Active Script List view and national prescription service conformance [older] — [Pulse+IT 14 Jun 2023](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/active-script-lists-national-prescription-service-conformance-in-bp-orchid-sp1/). Bp release notes call it "My Script List (MySL)"; Oxford fixed MySL "Date Created" to show the original prescription date — [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm). Spectra (Oct 2024) improved eScripts capability — [Pulse+IT 16 Oct 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-releases-spectra-version-featuring-improved-ai-scribe-and-escripts-capability/)
- Australian Immunisation Register: Oxford added "Nasal" route (FluMist only), effective 20 Oct 2025 — showing AIR code changes flow through Bp releases — [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm)
- NCSR: a dedicated NCSR Utility updated for Oxford SP1 — [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm)
- HPI-I/IHI: CDA eReferrals require the patient's IHI in demographics — [Bp KB: Argus setup (16 Feb 2024)](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/Integrations/Argus/InstallingArgus.htm)
- Secure messaging: Bp Premier supports provider-specific setup (e.g. ArgusSetup.exe, per-contact messaging provider). Argus was discontinued 31 Mar 2025 after acquisition by HealthLink, with users told to move to HealthLink — [Bp KB: Argus](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/Integrations/Argus/InstallingArgus.htm); [Bp KB: Secure Messaging and Online Directories](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/InstallAndUpgrade/SecureMessaging.htm)

### Inferences
- The AoB episode is the clearest dated example of national-policy volatility translating directly into vendor release risk and practice workload.
- Share by Default (1 Jul 2026) primarily obliges pathology/imaging providers, but GP software is the downstream consumer of those MHR documents; prescribing data may follow.

### Gaps
- No Bp-specific Share by Default or national eRequesting (ADHA eRequesting via FHIR) release note found; current Bp pathology flow is HL7 via messaging vendors. Treat Bp eRequesting conformance as unverified.
- HPI-O/HPI-I configuration pages in Bp not retrieved.
- HealthLink and Medical-Objects Bp Premier-specific setup pages not found (Medical-Objects/ReferralNet steps found only for Bp Allied).

## 3. Partners and integrations

### Takeaway
Third-party access is moving to one governed route: Halo Connect (FHIR/REST cloud API plus a local Halo Link connector), gated by Partner Network approval and per-practice opt-in. Partners span booking, payments, AI scribes, analytics, telehealth, PHNs and state health.

### Cited Findings
- Halo Connect: integration layer for approved Bp Partners; Halo Link installed automatically with Bp Premier; hosted on Azure in Australia; "stores no data" and cannot access practice data; included in the Bp subscription, partners may charge (FAQ updated 5 Apr 2023) [older] — [Bp KB: Halo Connect FAQ](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/Integrations/HaloConnect/HaloConnectFAQ.htm)
- Halo Connect supports Bp Premier and Zedmed; from December 2025 Bp Partners must use Halo Connect for Bp Premier and Bp cloud products; integrators pay a monthly per-practice fee, practices pay nothing; data may be temporarily cached for async retrieval (undated page) — [Halo Connect FAQ](https://haloconnect.io/faq). Search-engine summary of a Bp page states "From 1st January 2026, Halo Connect will be the only method for Partners to connect"; I could not open that page directly (404 on bestpracticesoftware.com/halo-connect), so Dec 2025 vs 1 Jan 2026 is unreconciled — [Bp KB Halo Connect FAQ (Spectra)](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/spectra/Integrations/HaloConnect/HaloConnectFAQ.htm)
- Bp invested in Halo Connect, built by the developers of Cubiko (22 Aug 2022) [older] — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-invests-in-halo-connect-fhir-powered-interoperability-platform/); [Healthcare IT News](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/anz/cubiko-developers-launch-cloud-based-interoperability-platform-halo-connect)
- Partner Network list (KB updated 30 Aug 2024) names ~61 partners including: HotDoc, HealthEngine, Pen CS, Cubiko, Outcome Health (POLAR), Primary Sense, Lyrebird Health, Heidi Health, Coviu, Telstra Health, BPAC Clinical Solutions, Doctors Control Panel, Jayex/Appointuit, MyHealth 1st, AutoMed Systems, Medmate, ConsultMed, MedRefer, Bupa, eHealth NSW, GHRANITE, Gold Coast PHN, Halo Connect itself. Partners on Halo Connect may show a second "(via Halo Connect)" entry the practice must tick — [Bp KB: Partner Network](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/Management/PartnerNetwork.htm). Note: brief's "130+ partners" figure not verified in this pass; the KB list is older and shorter.
- Online booking: HotDoc requires "HotDoc (via Halo Connect)" ticked; if disabled HotDoc can't read or write the database — [HotDoc support](https://support.hotdoc.com.au/hc/en-gb/articles/16100398145295). HealthEngine: Halo Connect route required for its integration to function from 31 Mar 2026 — [HealthEngine help](https://support.healthengine.com.au/en-au/help-centre-practice/enabling-healthengine-as-third-party-integration-for-best-practice-via-halo-connect-1). Bp launched its own Best Health Booking (25 Mar 2024) — [Pulse+IT](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-launches-best-health-booking-online-booking-management-solution/)
- Payments: HICAPS, Tyro, CommBank Smart Health (see section 2) — [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/spectra/PaymentIntegrations/PaymentIntegrations.htm); NAB on HICAPS-Bp integration — [NAB](https://www.nab.com.au/news/technology-ai/hicaps-best-practice)
- AI scribes: Bp-Lyrebird partnership announced 6 Feb 2024; Lyrebird Scribe integration shipped in Orchid SP2 (28 May 2024); improved in Spectra (16 Oct 2024); free Lyrebird tier for all Bp Premier customers launched 16 Feb 2026 — [Pulse+IT 6 Feb 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-to-integrate-lyrebird-healths-ai-transcription-tool/); [Pulse+IT 28 May 2024](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-releases-big-update-to-orchid-including-lyrebird-scribe-integration/); [Pulse+IT 16 Feb 2026](https://www.pulseit.news/australian-digital-health/best-practice-launches-free-lyrebird-ai-scribe-tier/); [Bp KB: Lyrebird Scribe](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/Integrations/LyrebirdHealth/LyrebirdScribeLP.htm). Heidi Health is a listed partner — [Bp KB Partner Network](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/Management/PartnerNetwork.htm). ### Inferences
- Halo Connect turns Bp from "a database partners read directly" into "a governed platform": better security and auditability, but a forced migration for every partner and a new opt-in step for every practice.
- Bp both partners with and competes with ecosystem players (Best Health Booking vs HotDoc/HealthEngine; embedded free Lyrebird vs Heidi), a platform-strategy tension worth naming in interview.

### Gaps
- Current Partner Network count (brief cites 130+) not confirmed from a dated primary source.
- No source found for practice counts or partner counts on Halo Connect.

## 4. Friction and failure propagation (dated examples)

### Takeaway
Failures propagate along three axes: policy changes forcing releases (AoB, Jun 2026), integration route changes forcing partner and practice action (Halo Connect, Dec 2025 to Mar 2026), and security hardening changing how everything connects (Database Connector, Aug to Oct 2026).

### Cited Findings
- AoB (Jun 2026): Government announced changes 18 Jun with ~8 working days to the 1 Jul start; Bp pulled its Oxford build; ~1,500 clinics already on it needed a July update to stop AoB workflows auto-activating; Bp estimated ~A$1m project cost; CTO said enduring assignment was still undefined; MSIA and other vendors shared frustration (22 Jun 2026) — [Medical Republic](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/?p=126623); [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm)
- Integration route change: partners must move to Halo Connect (Dec 2025 per Halo); HealthEngine set 31 Mar 2026 as cut-off; HotDoc warns its integration stops reading/writing if the "via Halo" entry is disabled — [Halo FAQ](https://haloconnect.io/faq); [HealthEngine](https://support.healthengine.com.au/en-au/help-centre-practice/enabling-healthengine-as-third-party-integration-for-best-practice-via-halo-connect-1); [HotDoc](https://support.hotdoc.com.au/hc/en-gb/articles/16100398145295)
- Upgrades vs integrations: Bp release notes advise practices with third-party integrations to confirm they still work after upgrading, as vendors may not yet have a compatible version — [Bp KB Saffron release notes](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/saffron/InstallAndUpgrade/ReleaseNotes/BpPremier_Saffron_ReleaseNotes.htm) [older]; device vendor LEMR documents Bp updates changing the device interface and breaking older integrations — [LEMR wiki](https://wiki.lemr.com/support/technical-support/bp-orchid-device-integration-compatibility); Bp asks partners to test pre-release builds — [Bp Partner Network newsletter](https://bpsoftware.net/?p=142334)
- Security: Oxford SP1 (19 Aug 2026; downloads page cites 4 Sep) adds the Best Practice Database Connector service giving each practice unique database passwords; Windows firewall rules auto-added but third-party firewalls may need manual configuration; workstations need admin approval — [Bp KB](https://kb.bestpracticesoftware.com/bppremier/oxford/WhatsNew/WhatsNewBpPrem.htm); [Bp downloads](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/bp-prem-downloads/). A CVE (CVE-2026-114119, hardcoded shared SQL Server admin credential, fixed in 1.14.1.1128; published ~21 Oct 2026) is reported by third-party CVE aggregators only (CVSS cited as 9.3 and 9.8 by different sites); not confirmed from Bp or an official advisory — [hol.org](https://hol.org/guard/security/cves/CVE-2026-114119-best-practice-software-bp-premier-before-11411128); [strix.ai](https://www.strix.ai/cve/CVE-2026-114119)
- Data update dependencies: releases have prerequisites (e.g. Oxford SP1 needs April 2026 data update; MyMedicare import tool needs Dec 2023 data update) — [Bp downloads](https://bestpracticesoftware.com/bp-prem-downloads/); [Bp KB Orchid SP1 R1](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/WhatsNew/BpPremier_OrchidSP1R1_ReleaseNotes.htm)
- Messaging vendor consolidation: Argus shut 31 Mar 2025 (acquired by HealthLink), forcing practices to switch secure messaging — [Bp KB](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/Orchid/Integrations/Argus/InstallingArgus.htm)
- eRx/MHR (2019) [older]: a PHN-distributed Bp document on eRx not sent to MyHR — [Brisbane South PHN PDF, 23 Jan 2019](https://bsPHN.org.au/documents/best_practice_-_erx_not_sent_to_myhr_final_23012019.pdf)

### Inferences
- The database-password change likely interacts with every on-premise integration that previously used shared credentials, which helps explain the push to Halo Connect (inference; not stated by Bp).
- A PM framing: Bp sits at the convergence of policy deadlines it doesn't control, partners it must not break, and practices running on-premise servers with uneven IT maturity.

### Gaps
- No Bp-confirmed post-mortem of a specific update bug breaking a named partner integration found.
- CVE details need confirmation from Bp or ACSC before being cited externally.
